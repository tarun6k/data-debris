# Terms of Use

**Data Debris** — last updated 14 September 2026. The current version lives at datadebris.app/terms.

## The product

Data Debris is a disk-space cleaner for macOS. Scanning and viewing results are free without limits. Cleaning, Auto Clean and Duplicates require a paid plan: 1 Year ($20) or 3 Years ($50), each covering 2 Macs.

## Purchases and refunds

Purchases are processed by Paddle as merchant of record; Paddle's checkout terms apply alongside these. Every purchase carries a 14-day refund, no questions asked — request it from the customer portal linked in Settings, or email support@datadebris.app.

Auto-renewal is off unless you turn it on at checkout. If it is on, we email you 14 days before the term renews. When a plan ends, the app returns to Free: scanning keeps working, your History and Undo keep working, and nothing on your disk is deleted or altered.

## What the app will and will not do

The app moves files to the Trash by default, journals every move before it happens, and offers Undo while items remain in the Trash. It never requires an administrator password and never touches system folders. You remain responsible for reviewing what you choose to remove; Data Debris shows the reason and consequence for every item before cleaning.
