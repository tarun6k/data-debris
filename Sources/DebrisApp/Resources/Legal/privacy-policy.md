# Privacy Policy

**Data Debris** — last updated 14 September 2026. The current version lives at datadebris.app/privacy.

## What the app can see

Data Debris scans your home folder, app caches in `~/Library` (when you grant Full Disk Access), and any external volumes you opt in explicitly. Scanning happens entirely on your Mac.

## What leaves your Mac

Nothing about your files. The app makes two kinds of network calls only:

- A signed update check against our update server.
- A license check against our license server when you buy, activate, or once a day to confirm an active license.

The license check sends your license key and an anonymous machine identifier (a one-way hash of your Mac's hardware UUID — the UUID itself never leaves your Mac). No file names, paths, sizes or scan results are ever transmitted. There is no telemetry by default.

## Payments

Purchases are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record. Paddle processes your payment details; we never see card numbers. We receive your email address from Paddle to deliver your license key and, if you choose auto-renewal, to remind you 14 days before a renewal.

## Your data, your control

- Scan results, history and settings are stored locally on your Mac and never uploaded.
- You can deactivate a Mac at any time from Settings, which removes its identifier from our license records.
- To delete your license records entirely, contact support@datadebris.app.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what the app does, the in-app Privacy & Security sheet is shown again before any further purchase.
